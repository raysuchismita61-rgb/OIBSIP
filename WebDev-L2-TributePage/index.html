Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India & People's President

Biography

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and leaders. Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he came from a humble family and achieved remarkable success through determination, hard work, and education.

He played a major role in India's missile development and space programmes. His contributions to defence technology earned him the title "Missile Man of India". His work inspired millions of students to dream big and pursue careers in science and technology.

In 2002, he became the 11th President of India. During his presidency, he was admired for his simplicity, kindness, and dedication to serving the nation. He regularly interacted with students and encouraged them to develop scientific thinking and strong values.

Even after completing his presidential term, Dr. Kalam continued to teach, write books, and motivate young people until his final day. His life remains a symbol of knowledge, humility, patriotism, and lifelong learning.

Major Achievements

1960

Graduated in Aerospace Engineering and joined DRDO.

1969

Joined ISRO and contributed to India's Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III).

1998

Played a key role in India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests.

2002

Became the 11th President of India.

2015

Passed away while delivering a lecture to students, inspiring them until his final moments.
